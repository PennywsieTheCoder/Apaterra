# Apa Terra product roadmap

## Hair Profile — future personalization update

The Hair Profile interface was removed from the customer profile for the current release because its saved selections did not yet change the shopping experience. The existing `profiles.hair_needs` field is intentionally retained so the feature can return without a data migration.

Reintroduce Hair Profile when it supports a complete personalized experience:

- collect hair goals through a short onboarding quiz;
- rank and explain relevant product recommendations;
- preselect useful Shop filters without hiding the full catalogue;
- build suggested wash-day and maintenance routines;
- tailor care guidance and optional restock notifications;
- let customers review or change their answers from their profile;
- measure whether recommendations lead to useful product discovery.

Do not restore it as a standalone checklist. Its selections should have an immediate, visible effect elsewhere in the app.
